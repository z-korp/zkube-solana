Solana logomark

mark.svg, mark-white.svg and mark-black.svg are the Solana logomark as the Solana Foundation issues it: the
gradient mark from https://solana.com/branding and the one-colour white and black marks from the brand kit that
page links to. Their bytes are unchanged; sources.json records where each was downloaded and its SHA-256.
mark.png, mark-white.png and mark-black.png are proportional rasterizations of those files, 128 px tall, with
nothing added.

The mark is the Solana Foundation's trademark, not this project's, and its use here is under the Foundation's
brand guidelines (https://solana.com/branding), which govern over anything in this repository. zKube shows it
after an amount to say the amount is in SOL, and claims no ownership of it and no affiliation with or
endorsement by the Foundation.

How it is used here, as those guidelines ask:
- Never recoloured, tinted, outlined, shadowed, skewed or cropped. Each surface takes one of the three issued
  versions: the gradient or the white on a dark surface, the black on a light one.
- Kept clear of everything else by half its height on every side, the amount before it included.
- It is not a part of any skin: no skin restyles or replaces it, and it has no slot in the skin catalog.
