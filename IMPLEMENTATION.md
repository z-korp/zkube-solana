## Product Contract

zKube is a fully on-chain puzzle game with two connected loops:

- **Campaign (PvE):** ten-level themed maps with a boss, authored objectives, bonuses, mutators, combos, scores, and zero-to-three-star level ratings.
- **Daily Arena:** a daily endless-mode asynchronous competition. Each new row comes from a fresh MagicBlock VRF result; there is no precomputed deterministic piece sequence.

Progression and conversion rules:

- Map 1 is unlocked for every player.
- Clearing Map 1 unlocks Daily Arena eligibility.
- Perfecting a map unlocks the next map for free.
- Otherwise, the next map can be unlocked with Stars or USDC.
- Stars are non-transferable points stored in the player's program account, not an SPL token.
- Stars are earned from best-result improvements and capped daily/weekly quests. The original 24 achievements award non-spendable XP, not Stars, so trophy progression cannot inflate the map-unlock economy.
- Daily quest budget is exactly 5 Stars: three one-Star quests plus a two-Star all-three finisher.
- Weekly quest budget is exactly 10 Stars: two five-Star quests. A fully completed seven-day quest cycle therefore issues at most 45 Stars.
- Campaign level bests issue at most 300 Stars across ten maps; repeatable quests become the long-term progression source after campaign completion. No guessed 200-Star achievement cap is imposed.
- The canonical achievement catalog issues at most 6,700 non-transferable XP across 24 one-time trophies.
- A player may redeem Stars for at most one Daily Arena attempt per day.
- A player may buy unlimited Daily Arena attempts at the configured USDC price; the initial model is 1 USDC.
- Only a player's best finalized attempt occupies a leaderboard position.

Initial Daily Arena business rules:

- Entry waterfall: 90% guaranteed prize pool and 10% protocol rake.
- Rake split: 25% team, 25% paymaster reserve, 50% protocol treasury.
- Once the paymaster reserve reaches its configured cap, its overflow routes to the treasury.
- Sponsor deposits and realized treasury yield may boost later prize pools.
- Earned prizes remain claimable for 90 days after challenge finalization. Only an explicit post-deadline forfeiture transition may remove an unclaimed liability; the destination must remain a segregated reward/rollover account rather than protocol revenue.
- Active contest liabilities are not deployed into yield strategies.
- Yield work is limited to protocol-owned treasury/reserve capital and requires a separate audited adapter iteration.

## Architecture

### Authority boundaries

The browser is presentation and transaction orchestration only. It never computes an authoritative row, move result, score, level result, reward, or leaderboard result.

| Boundary             | Authoritative responsibilities                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Solana base layer    | Player identity and progression, content versions, Stars, entitlements, quests/achievements, Daily definitions and entries, USDC custody/accounting, finalized receipts, leaderboards, claims, treasury policy.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| MagicBlock public ER | Active campaign or Daily run: grid, visible next row, objectives, mutators, bonuses, score/combo/moves, VRF request state, action/VRF commitment chain, final run projection.                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Sponsorship relay    | Statelessly validate an allowlisted transaction shape, read/consume program-owned sponsorship allowance state, co-sign as fee payer, and submit. Solana requires the fee payer signature before program execution, so zero-SOL base transactions cannot be both backend-free and player-unfunded. The relay has no mutable quota database, gameplay authority, or treasury authority. |
| Index/query layer    | Non-authoritative history, leaderboard pagination, analytics, notifications, and cached presentation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |

### Durable base-layer accounts

- `ProtocolConfig`: version, authorities/multisig, pause flags, supported payment mint and token program, paymaster, content/progress versions, treasury ledger reference, and yield-policy reference.
- `GovernanceProposal`: monotonically identified, program-owned timelocked action with creation, execution, expiry, cancellation, and proposer evidence. Emergency pause is immediate; unpause and sensitive policy changes are delayed.
- `TreasuryLedger`: immutable cumulative classification of rake, team/paymaster/treasury distributions, map revenue, swept revenue, forfeitures retained for rewards, strategy deposits/repayments/outstanding principal, realized yield, yield allocation, and realized losses.
- `YieldStrategyPolicy`: program-owned, disabled-by-default, monotonically versioned allowlist for one adapter program, market, reserve, and receipt mint plus principal, exposure, liquidity, slippage, loss, realized-yield routing, deposit-enable, and emergency-exit controls.
- `PlayerProfile`: owner, Stars balance and lifetime counters, non-spendable achievement XP, Daily eligibility, quest cadence counters, achievement flags, next run id.
- `ProgressCatalog`: immutable governance-published version of achievement/quest metrics, thresholds, cadence/rotation rules, and bounded Star rewards.
- `QuestClaims`: per-player/per-progress-version daily and weekly claim bitmaps with independent cadence epochs.
- `CampaignProgress`: owner, unlocked-map bitmap, paid-unlock bitmap, packed best stars per level/map, cleared/perfected flags.
- `CampaignCatalog`: immutable/versioned map metadata, unlock prices, level catalog references, enabled flags.
- `LevelCatalog`: immutable/versioned objective, move, difficulty, boss, bonus, mutator, and star-threshold rules.
- `RunShell`: unique `(owner, run_id)` identity, mode, content/rules version, settlement target, session authority, delegated validator, lifecycle status.
- `RunReceipt`: copied-back immutable final projection: owner, mode, rules version, final score, completion facts, move count, action hash, VRF-chain hash, timestamps/counters, consumed flag.
- `DailyChallenge`: day id, entry window, play deadline, rules version, entry price, prize/rake policy snapshot, sponsor funding, liabilities, status.
- `DailyPlayer`: `(challenge, player)` aggregate with free-ticket use, paid attempt count, finalized attempt count, best receipt/score, rank/claim state.
- Later financial accounts: contest vault/token account, allocation ledger, paymaster reserve, protocol treasury, reward reserve, strategy position.

All identities are PDA-derived. All versions and monetary amounts use fixed-width integers. USDC amounts use the mint's integer base units; floating point is forbidden. All additions, subtractions, splits, and conversions use checked arithmetic and preserve explicit conservation equations.

### Delegated active-run account

`ActiveRun` is created on base, initialized as a complete shell, and then delegated. It contains only data required to execute and prove one attempt:

- owner, run id, mode, rules/content version, session authority, lifecycle status;
- campaign map/level context or Daily challenge/attempt context;
- 10x8 grid, visible next row, score, combos, moves, objective progress;
- active/passive mutators, bonus inventory/uses, difficulty state;
- VRF request counter, pending request id/status, callback deadline/retry state;
- rolling action commitment and rolling VRF-result commitment;
- final projection used to create the base-layer receipt.

There is no reusable global game account and no hidden future row sequence. A run id can be settled exactly once.

### Run lifecycle

1. On base, validate eligibility/payment and prepare `RunShell`, `ActiveRun`, and, for Daily, the attempt authorization.
2. The paymaster validates and sponsors the allowlisted setup transaction.
3. Select an ER through the Magic Router, delegate `ActiveRun`, and resolve its actual ER endpoint from delegation status.
4. Authorize a scoped, expiring browser session key. The wallet remains the owner; the session key can only call explicitly allowed run instructions for the bound run.
5. On ER, initialize the run and request enough VRF output to expose the first playable grid/next row.
6. Each move validates ownership/session, expected move number, indices, current phase, rules snapshot, and grid legality before applying the Rust engine transition.
7. When another row is required, transition to `AwaitingVrf`. The oracle-authorized callback binds randomness to `(run, request_counter, rules_version)`, rejects duplicate/out-of-order callbacks, derives one row, updates the VRF commitment, and returns to `Playing`.
8. Finish seals the final projection and prevents further moves or randomness callbacks.
9. Commit and undelegate copy the result to base. A Magic Action consumes it idempotently into a `RunReceipt` and updates campaign progression or the Daily best result.
10. After proof of settlement, close transient accounts and return rent to the configured recipient.

Current implementation:

- Finalized contests can distribute only accrued rake: 25% team, up to 25% paymaster reserve, and all remainder to the treasury vault. The remainder includes the 50% treasury share, capped paymaster overflow, and rounding dust.
- Prize liabilities remain in the contest vault and cannot enter rake distribution.
- Protocol initialization now decodes and validates the configured payment mint and every vault. Paymaster, treasury, reward, and payment vaults must be mint-matched token accounts controlled by the protocol PDA; the team vault remains the external payout destination. This removes the former risk of accepting internally unusable vaults.
- All five custody vault addresses must also be nonzero and pairwise distinct. Protocol v1 pins a six-decimal canonical SPL Token mint domain; Token-2022 extension-bearing payment assets are rejected until an explicit extension-aware accounting version exists.
- A program-owned `TreasuryLedger` atomically records every routed rake base unit, map sale, revenue sweep, and expired-prize inflow. Conservation checks require each rake and revenue split to balance exactly. Forfeitures remain classified as reward-reserve inflow, never revenue or yield.
- Map revenue accumulates in the protocol-owned payment vault and can be swept permissionlessly according to the timelocked `revenue_reward_bps` policy. The activation configuration routes principal to treasury (`0` reward bps); future reward funding is intended to come from realized yield or separately classified reward inflows, not silently from active liabilities.
- Governance supports immediate authority-signed protocol and yield-deposit emergency pauses, but unpause, paymaster/allowance/cap changes, yield configuration/activation, content- or progress-version activation, revenue routing, governance-timing changes, and pending-authority nomination require a program-owned proposal, bounded timelock, bounded execution window, and explicit execution. Authority transfer completes only when the nominated authority signs acceptance; the authority can be a multisig signer.
- The Settings surface publicly decodes protocol, ledger, and token-vault accounts, verifies program ownership/mint relationships before display, and exposes balances, lifetime classifications, governance delay, pause status, revenue routing, strategy principal, and realized yield.
- `YieldStrategyPolicy` initializes at strategy-policy version zero with no adapter and deposits disabled. A timelocked proposal must advance the version and pin the exact adapter program, market, reserve, and receipt mint, and is bounded on-chain to at most 50% exposure, at least 50% liquid reserve, at most 1% slippage, and at most 10% recognized loss. These are hard safety ceilings, not a production recommendation. Enabling deposits is a separate timelocked action; immediate emergency pause disables new deposits and raises the emergency-exit flag.
- Both proposal creation and execution bind the canonical program-owned `TreasuryLedger`. Strategy reconfiguration is rejected while `strategy_principal` is nonzero, preventing governance from orphaning a deployed position. Public clients decode the policy and expose its state and limits.
- Strategy ledger transitions now record lifetime deposits, lifetime principal repayments, outstanding principal, realized yield, and realized loss atomically. The independently checkable identity is `lifetime deposits = outstanding principal + lifetime principal repaid + realized losses`; realized yield is never counted as principal repayment.
- Realized yield defaults to 100% reward-reserve routing, matching the product contract, and governance may change the basis-point split only through the timelock. A permissionless allocation instruction transfers only ledger-recorded, not-yet-processed realized yield from the treasury vault to the reward vault and separately records any treasury-retained share. It has no authority to classify funds as yield.
- There is deliberately no external strategy deposit, withdrawal, valuation, or harvest CPI yet. The control plane still cannot deploy treasury custody or fabricate realized yield, and `emergency_exit` remains a state signal rather than an implemented withdrawal path.
